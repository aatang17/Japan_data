How many nights did guests stay in Japanese hotels last month?

Definition

A guest night is one guest staying one night. The Japan Tourism Agency's Accommodation Survey counts them every month across hotels, inns and other lodging in Japan, Japanese and foreign guests together, and publishes a preliminary figure about a month after the month ends, revised later.

The survey's coverage and estimation changed from January 2026; readings before and after that point are not on exactly the same basis, and the data page states the break. The comparison with a year earlier is calculated from the published counts.

Guest nights by prefecture and by guest nationality are on the data page.

How to Cite

The full series, every earlier release and a CSV with its sources in the header are on the data page. Values can be read as they stood on any earlier date through the API; guidance for quoting is in llms.txt.

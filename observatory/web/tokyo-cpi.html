What is Tokyo's CPI inflation rate?

Definition

The Statistics Bureau of Japan publishes a preliminary Consumer Price Index for the 23 wards of Tokyo at the end of the month it covers, about three weeks before the national index. It is the first official reading of Japanese inflation for each month and is widely read as a guide to the national figure, which follows a similar path.

The rate quoted is the headline index, all items, compared with the same month a year earlier, with core (less fresh food) and core-core (less fresh food and energy) beside it. Same base (2025 = 100) and same definitions as the national index. The preliminary reading is revised once, when the final Tokyo figure is published with the national release.

Rates are calculated from the published index levels and can differ from the Bureau's published rate by up to 0.1 percentage points; the method is stated under Show calculation.

  • Cross-shareholdings (政策保有株式) Live · partial coverage

    Policy shareholdings exactly as disclosed in annual securities reports: what each company holds, who holds it back, the stated reason for every holding, and how fast the unwind is running. Financial sector first; coverage is stated on every figure and grows as filings are extracted.

  • Boards & pay (役員の状況・報酬) Live

    Every director of every listed company — name, role, age, board seat and shareholding — with officer remuneration by category and every individual disclosed at ¥100m or more, from the same annual securities reports. Five fiscal years, so a board can be read against itself.

How This Is Built

  • Primary sources only. Consumer prices come from the Statistics Bureau of Japan via e-Stat; cross-shareholdings come from companies' own filings on EDINET. Every source file is archived with its SHA-256 checksum before anything is parsed.
  • Official and calculated are never blurred. Index levels and filed figures are published exactly as they came. Anything this platform computes — year-over-year rates, contributions, breadth, unwind pace — carries its formula instead of a badge.
  • Missing stays missing. A gap is shown as an em dash and exported as empty, never as zero.
  • Coverage is stated, not implied. Where a dataset is partial, every aggregate names its denominator.
  • Every view has an address. The URL carries the full state of a chart or table, so anything you publish stays reproducible. See the Methodology for formulas, limitations, and attribution.
Sources: Statistics Bureau of Japan, via e-Stat · company filings via EDINET (Financial Services Agency). Index values and filed figures are official; rates of change and aggregates are calculated on this platform. Methodology

How many foreign visitors came to Japan last month?

Definition

The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) publishes an estimate of foreign visitor arrivals around the third week of the following month, from Ministry of Justice immigration counts. It counts foreign nationals entering Japan and excludes those who live in Japan; crew members are also excluded.

The latest month is an estimate and is replaced by a final count about two months later. The comparison with a year earlier is calculated from the published counts.

Arrivals by market, the recovery against 2019 and each market's contribution to the change are on the data page.

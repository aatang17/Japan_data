Shareholder Register

Who owns each listed Japanese company: the named holders in the annual report's 大株主の状況 table, and the whole register split by investor category — foreign institutions, individuals, banks and insurers, other companies. Exactly as filed. Nominee trust-bank accounts are marked, because they hold for others and topping a register is not owning a company.

The Register

The filing's own 大株主の状況 table. Names, addresses, share counts and percentages are official statistics as filed. The percentage is of shares in issue excluding treasury — the filing's own denominator. Holder type is our reading of the name, not a filed field.

The Whole Register, by Investor Type

The filing's 所有者別状況 table: every shareholder on the register, not only the largest. Percentages here are of all issued shares — a different denominator from the table above, and the two are never netted.

Year by Year

The same company against itself, one row per filing. A missing year means that filing was not extractable, not that the company stopped filing.

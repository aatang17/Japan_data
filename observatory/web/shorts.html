Short Positions

Every disclosed short position in a Japanese listed company. A holder reaching 0.5% of an issuer's shares must report it, and the exchange publishes the report the next business day — holder, issuer, size, and the day the position was measured. The mirror of the 5% long filings, and the one record here that nobody can rebuild: JPX deletes each daily file after about twelve business days.

Source: Japan Exchange Group — 空売り残高に関する情報 (Information on Outstanding Short Selling Positions), published each business day. Ratios and share counts are official, exactly as published; the ratio is shown as the percentage JPX's own PDF prints, where the workbook writes the same number as a decimal fraction. Whether a position is still open, and a company's total across its holders, are calculated here and carry their formula. Each daily workbook is archived with its SHA-256 before parsing. Only positions of 0.5% or more are disclosed at all, so a company's total is the disclosed short — always less than its true short interest.

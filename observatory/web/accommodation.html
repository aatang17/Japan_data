Guest Nights and Occupancy

Guest Nights by Month

Person-nights exactly as the Agency published them. Guest nights are strongly seasonal, so the second view separates Japanese from foreign guests, which move on different cycles, and the third indexes each month against the same month of 2019 — the last full year before the border closed.

Room Occupancy by Hotel Type

The published room occupancy rate, in percent. A rate is a ratio, so it is differenced rather than divided and is never averaged across areas here. Business and city hotels carry the inbound trade; ryokan and company lodging move on domestic demand.

Demand Mix

The same guest nights read three other ways. Purpose classifies the property, not the guest: a property is counted as leisure when at least half of its guests stayed for leisure. Residence separates guests from inside the prefecture from those who travelled to it. Property size is published from January 2026 only, when the survey changed from stratifying by employee count to stratifying by room count.

Source: Japan Tourism Agency, Accommodation Survey. Guest nights and occupancy rates are official statistics as published; growth rates, shares, recovery indices and contributions are calculated from them. Methodology

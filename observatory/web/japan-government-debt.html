How large is Japan's government debt?

Definition

The Ministry of Finance publishes the central government's outstanding debt at each quarter-end: government bonds, borrowings and financing bills. This is the figure usually quoted as Japan's national debt. It is a gross figure — it is not reduced by the government's own financial assets — and it excludes local government debt.

The value here is in hundreds of millions of yen as published; the sentence above converts it to trillions. Changes on the quarter and on the year are calculated from the published values.

The composition of the debt, tax receipts and the budget are on the public finance page.

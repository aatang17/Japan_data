Who Depends on Whom

Every listed Japanese company must name any customer worth ten percent or more of its revenue, and state the amount. This is that disclosure, assembled — the only company-to-company revenue link in the public record.

Look Up a Buyer

Type a customer's name to see every Japanese company that discloses depending on it, and how much of each supplier's revenue that relationship represents. Names match the text as each filer wrote it, so try more than one spelling — Samsung appears as three different strings because three filers wrote it three ways.

Buyers With the Most Japanese Suppliers

Ranked by how many listed Japanese companies name them, then by the revenue those companies book from them. A name appearing twice in different spellings is counted twice — the platform never merges names the filers wrote differently.

Most Dependent Suppliers

Every company that names a customer, ranked by the share of its own revenue those named relationships account for. A company absent from this list has no customer at or above the ten percent threshold — which is not the same as having a diversified book, only that nothing crossed the line it must report.

Source: company filings on EDINET (Financial Services Agency of Japan). Customer names and amounts are exactly as filed; the dependence percentages are calculated from them and carry their formula. Methodology

AGM Votes

How every resolution at a Japanese shareholder meeting was actually voted — and, for board elections, how much support each named director received. Companies must file this within days of the meeting. It is the only public record that puts a number on a director's mandate, and the low end of that number is where the disagreements are.

Source: 臨時報告書 (extraordinary reports) via EDINET, Financial Services Agency. Vote counts are voting rights (個), one per trading unit — not shares, and not comparable with the share counts on the 5% Filings or Register pages. Approval percentages are exactly as each company filed them; they are not recomputed here, because issuers routinely stop counting attending votes once the outcome is settled and do not publish the base they used. Every raw file is archived with its SHA-256. Coverage begins in April 2024, when these filings first became reachable — earlier ones had already left EDINET's public inspection window.

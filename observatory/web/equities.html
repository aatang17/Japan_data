Japan Equities

Ownership, governance and financials of listed Japan, from companies' own filings on EDINET. Figures are exactly as filed; aggregates carry their formula.

  • Cross-Shareholdings (政策保有株式) Live · partial coverage

    Policy shareholdings as disclosed in annual securities reports: holder, issuer, stated reason and pace of unwind.

  • Shareholder Register (大株主の状況) Live

    Named large holders and the whole register by investor category, with nominee trust-bank accounts marked.

  • 5% Filings (大量保有報告書) Live

    Every large-shareholding report: filer, issuer, stake movement, and whether the filer reserves the right to make important proposals.

  • Boards & Pay (役員の状況・報酬) Live

    Every director by role, age, seat and shareholding; officer pay by category and every individual disclosed at ¥100m or more. Five fiscal years.

  • Buybacks (自己株券買付状況報告書) Live

    Authorised amount, monthly execution and cancellation per programme, from the monthly status reports. The archive starts where ours does.

  • Facilities & Land (主要な設備の状況) Live

    Every disclosed plant, office, store and landholding, geocoded, with land at book value, floor area and headcount by site.

  • Financials (財務諸表・主要な経営指標) Live

    Balance sheet, income statement and cash flows line by line as tagged, plus each filer's own five-year key figures.

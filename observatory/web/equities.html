Japan Equities

Ownership and governance of listed Japan, extracted from companies' own filings on EDINET — who holds what, who sits where, what is being bought back and what is being unwound. Figures are exactly as filed; anything aggregated or compared across filings carries its formula on the page.

  • Cross-Shareholdings (政策保有株式) Live · partial coverage

    Policy shareholdings exactly as disclosed in annual securities reports: what each company holds, who holds it back, the stated reason for every holding, and how fast the unwind is running. Coverage is stated on every figure and grows as filings are extracted.

  • Shareholder Register (大株主の状況) Live

    Who owns each listed company: the named large holders in the annual report's register table, and the whole register split by investor category — foreign institutions, individuals, banks and insurers, other companies. Nominee trust-bank accounts are marked, because they hold for someone else.

  • 5% Filings (大量保有報告書) Live

    Every large-shareholding report filed on a Japanese listed company: who crossed 5%, in whom, how the stake has moved since, and whether the filer states it may make important proposals to the board — the filed line between a passive stake and a campaign.

  • Boards & Pay (役員の状況・報酬) Live

    Every director of every listed company — name, role, age, board seat and shareholding — with officer remuneration by category and every individual disclosed at ¥100m or more. Five fiscal years, so a board can be read against itself.

  • Buybacks (自己株券買付状況報告書) Live

    What each company said it would buy back, what it actually bought, and whether the shares were cancelled — from the monthly status report filed while a programme runs. EDINET deletes these filings after about a year, so the record starts where our archive does and can only grow.

  • Facilities & Land (主要な設備の状況) Live

    Every plant, office, store and landholding disclosed in the major facilities section of annual securities reports — geocoded and mapped, with land at book value, floor area and headcount by site.

On the roadmap: ranked unwind screens across holders, and overlap of cross-shareholdings with disclosed activist positions.

Source: company filings via EDINET (Financial Services Agency). Filed figures are official; aggregates, averages and year-on-year comparisons are calculated on this platform. Methodology

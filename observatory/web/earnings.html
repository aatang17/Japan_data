Since the statutory quarterly reports were abolished in 2024 this is the only source for first- and third-quarter results — and the exchange deletes each disclosure after about a month.

Other Disclosures

Source: company disclosures on TDnet, the Tokyo Stock Exchange's Timely Disclosure network — the Summary of each 決算短信 (earnings release). Amounts, per-share figures and year-on-year changes are official, exactly as each company tagged them; results are cumulative from the start of the fiscal year. Progress against the company's forecast, which release is current for a period, and the fiscal period read from the headline are calculated here and carry their formula. Each package is archived with its SHA-256 before parsing. TDnet keeps a disclosure public for about a month, so this record begins on 10 July 2026 and cannot be back-filled.

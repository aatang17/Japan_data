How many Japanese government bonds does the Bank of Japan hold?

Definition

The Bank of Japan publishes its balance sheet in its accounts, and the figure here is the line for Japanese government bonds (JGBs) at each month-end, in hundreds of millions of yen as published; the sentence above converts it to trillions.

The Bank's JGB holdings grew from 2013 under quantitative and qualitative easing, when it bought bonds at a pace that made it the largest single holder. Purchases were reduced from 2024, and the stock now falls as bonds mature faster than they are replaced. The distance from the peak is stated above.

The Bank of Japan's own data are the source; the credit line the Bank requires is at the foot of this page and in every export.

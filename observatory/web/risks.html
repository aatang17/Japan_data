Business Risks

The risks each listed company says could materially affect its business, in its own words, from the 事業等のリスク section of its annual securities report. Open a company to read its risks item by item and see what is new since last year, or search every company's section for a word.

Source: company filings on EDINET (Financial Services Agency of Japan) — 事業等のリスク, annual securities reports; each company's latest report on file. Text as filed; the item it falls in is calculated (see a company's Show calculation).
